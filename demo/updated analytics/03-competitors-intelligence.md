## 1. Purpose

Compares the brand with its competitors on three fronts:
1. **AI visibility**: how often the brand and competitors are mentioned by AI models (share of voice and mention counts over time, Gemini and ChatGPT).
2. **Search strength**: competitor domain metrics from Semrush (organic traffic, keywords, backlinks, referring domains, authority score).
3. **Traffic and keyword mix**: share of organic traffic by domain, and branded vs non-branded keyword split by domain.

An AI-written insight and recommended actions summarise the competitive position.

- Pattern: "{Brand} holds just {n}% traffic share and an authority score of {n}, lagging significantly behind leaders {Competitor A} ({n}%) and {Competitor B} ({n}%)."

## 5. Data sources and API calls

| Data block | Call | Input |
|---|---|---|
| Mentions chart (and its brand list) | `POST fetch-competitor-mentions` | `{ "brandName": "{Brand}" }`, also with `dateRangeDays: 7` and with `startDate`/`endDate` (`{YYYY-MM-DD}`) per date range |
| Share of Voice chart | `POST rpc/get_share_of_voice_window` | `{ "p_brand_name": "{Brand}", "p_days": {7\|14\|30}, "p_offset": {n}, "p_limit": 1000 }`, called repeatedly with `p_offset` 0, 1000, 2000 … until all rows are loaded |
| Brand lookup | `GET brands` (select `name`, `username_api`, filter by brand name) | Brand name |
| Authority Score table | `POST fetch-semrush-competitor-data` | Brand context |
| Domain by Traffic share | `POST fetch-traffic-share-data` | Brand context |
| Branded vs non-branded keywords by domain | `POST fetch-keywords-by-domain` | Brand context |
| Insight line and RECOMMENDED ACTIONS | `POST generate-page-insights` | `{ "page": "CompetitorsIntelligence", "brand": "{Brand}", "source": "semrush competitor metrics, mentions chart, traffic share", "signals": { "user_brand", "user_domain", "user_authority_score", "tracked_brands_count", … } }` |
| Usage tracking | `POST user_page_sessions` | `page_path: "/analytics/competitors"`, `page_label: "Competitors"`, `user_id` |

Shell calls (roles, subscription, notifications) are listed in [00-global-layout.md](00-global-layout.md) section 5.

## 6. Behaviour rules

- **Brand scoped:** the page is built around the brand selected in the top bar; competitors come from the brand's tracked competitor set and the Semrush data.
- **Own brand:** the **Clear** action keeps it selected, and it is drawn first in tooltips and the legend. Clicking its own row in the filter list **does deselect it** (the count drops by one, e.g. "10 of {total}" to "9 of {total}"), so it is only protected from **Clear**.
- **Date ranges** are counted back from the most recent date with data, not from today (the sample showed a 7-day window of Sep 14 to Sep 20 while the current date was in November).
- **Two independent chart controls:** each of the two line charts has its own brand filter, range and model toggle.
- **Share of Voice values** are each brand's percentage share of that day's mentions across all tracked brands (per the ⓘ tooltip). The data call loads all rows for the window, so the selected-brand filter is applied in the browser.
- **Authority and traffic data** are Semrush figures that are refreshed periodically ("Last updated" note), so they do not change with the chart date ranges.
- **Generated content:** the insight line and action cards come from `generate-page-insights`; wording, number of cards, categories and priorities can differ between loads.
